How Align trains your posture

Three steps, and about fifteen minutes of wear a day to start.

01

Stick it on

Place Align on your upper back with a reusable adhesive, or clip on the necklace if you'd rather skip adhesive entirely.

02

Calibrate

Sit or stand up straight and hold still for three seconds. Align learns what upright feels like for your body.

03

Feel the buzz

Slouch, and Align gives a gentle vibration to remind you. Over a couple of weeks, your muscles start holding the posture on their own.

Two ways to wear it

Adhesive

Reusable, skin-safe adhesives hold Align flat against your upper back. Included in both kits, with 10 adhesives to start — each one lasts several wears.

Necklace

Clip Align onto the included necklace and wear it like a pendant — no adhesive, easy to take on and off. Comes standard with the Pro Kit.

Questions people ask before buying

Align calibrates to your upright posture when you first put it on, then uses motion sensing to detect when your back curves away from that position. When it does, it gives a gentle vibration.

Up to 12 days on a single charge with typical daily wear. It charges over USB-C, so a cable you already own will usually work.

The device is small and light, and the adhesive is skin-safe and reusable. If you'd rather skip adhesive entirely, the necklace option clips on instead.

Align is splash-resistant, so sweat and light rain are fine. It isn't fully waterproof, so avoid submerging it and keep the USB-C port dry.

30 days to try it and send it back for a full refund if it's not for you. Every device also ships with a 1-year warranty.
